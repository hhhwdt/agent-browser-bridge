# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## What is expected

- Be direct about the code and the ideas, not about the person. "This silently returns success" is
  useful; "you clearly did not think about this" is not.
- Assume good faith, and give people room to be wrong in public — that is what a code review is for.
- Respect the tool's threat model. This project drives a real browser holding someone's logged-in
  session. Advice that amounts to "just grant all sites and enable everything" is not helpful here,
  and neither is asking anyone to paste cookies, tokens or session storage from a browser that is not
  theirs.
- English and Chinese are both welcome. The maintainer writes both, and the docs ship in both.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, personal or political attacks, insults, and sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language or imagery relating to age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
  expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
  race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information — including logs, hostnames, cookies or session data from
  a browser that is not yours — without their permission.
- Encouraging someone to run this tool against systems or accounts they are not authorised to use.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews reports and decides what to do. Responses escalate with severity and
repetition: a private warning, then a temporary block from the repository, then a permanent ban.
Reasons are given to the person involved. Public call-outs are not part of the process.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately: use the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile
([@hhwdt](https://github.com/hhhwdt)), or GitHub's private reporting on this repository. Do not open a
public issue about someone's behaviour. Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter's privacy
is respected.
